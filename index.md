# Privacy Policy - Forzora

**Last updated: 6 October 2026**

Forzora ("the app") is a gym workout log published by **AppVita Studio**.
It helps you build workout programs, log your sets, time your rests and track
your progress. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## 1. Your training data stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app - programs, exercises, sets, weights,
repetitions, notes, goals, personal records, body weight and body measurements -
is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no
servers that receive it. There is no account and no registration.

The only time this data leaves your device is when **you** choose to export or
share it (JSON backup, CSV export, shared program file or workout summary,
through the Android share sheet or file picker): in that case you decide where
it goes.

## 2. Android backup

If you have enabled Google backup on your phone, Android may include the app's
database and settings in your personal backup, so that they can be restored on a
new device. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your Google
account settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in the Android
settings.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Forzora is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**.
Ads are shown only in browsing screens and never during a workout.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**None of the training or body data you enter in the app is ever shared with the
advertising network.** The two things are kept completely separate.

You can watch an optional rewarded video to hide ads for 24 hours.

## 4. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
app shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)
**before any ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised
advertising. If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change your choice at any time from **Profile > About and privacy > Ad
privacy choices** inside the app.

## 5. Notifications and alarms

The app can show the rest timer countdown, alert you when a rest period is over
and, if you turn them on, remind you of your training days. All of this is
scheduled **locally on your device**; nothing passes through our servers,
because we have none. You can refuse or revoke the notification permission at
any time in the Android settings.

## 6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise
location, health records or any of your training data. The app does not request
the location, camera, microphone or body sensor permissions and uses no analytics
SDK of its own. Crash reports are saved only on your device and are sent to us
only if you decide to email them.

## 7. Not a medical app

Forzora is a training log. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition
and does not replace a doctor or a qualified coach. Body measurements are stored
as you enter them and are not evaluated.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

## 9. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Access and portability**: export your data at any time from Profile >
  Backup, import and export (JSON backup, CSV)
- **Deletion**: delete any item inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove
  everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings,
  and change your consent from the app's ad privacy choices

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise
your rights directly with Google through the links in section 3.
